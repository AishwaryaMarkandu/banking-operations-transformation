How I approached the analysis

1. Data quality. I processed the CFPB dataset in chunks and kept the raw source untouched.

2. Business framing. I focused on customer pain, operational responsiveness and recurring issue patterns.

3. Operational KPIs. I measured complaint volume, timely response and response-delay signals.

4. Prioritisation. I combined four dimensions into a transparent score.

5. Transformation lens. I translated the analytical signals into areas for further investigation.
